I've been trying to buy things that make my living space intutive and functional.

Over the years, I've accumulated a large collection of cables. I tried separating them by size and later by type by putting them in different boxes. Over time, the boxes would inexplicably move away from each other, become overfilled, and I could never find the cable I needed quickly. The quickest solution was to dump them all in a steel cookie box and place the box in a drawer so that the cables would have somewhere to go in case they overfilled the box. Finding a particular cable meant picking one up at random, inspecting the ends to ensure that it is comptaible with my needs, and rummaging through the box in the drawer if I didn't get lucky the first time.

After watching a TikTok on filling tiny go away pouches for your essentials that you could just "grab and go", I bought [these zipper pouches](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0F6MH8KL7?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title&th=1).

The first comment on the product was a guy raving about how useful these pouches were to organize his cables 💡

A collection avoids becoming a heap through organization.

My cable drawer is now organized into 4 pouches (usb-c, lightening, bricks and adaptors, and miscellaneous). It's so much easier to find the exact cable I need.

![My organized cable drawer](/postImages/small-living-adjustments-1.jpeg)

Organizing begets more organizing. While putting the cables in their bags, I noticed that it was hard to coil them up in neat bundles. I was using metal wires to twist around the cables and when I ran out of those, started using tape. Looked ugly and made the cables super sticky.

So, I got [these velcro cable ties](amazon.com/dp/B081HH5X61?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title). Now, every cable gets its own permanent velcro tie and so that it's easy to stow away, use, and carry for travel.

![An example of a corded cable](/postImages/small-living-adjustments-2.jpeg)

Sometimes, the smallest tweaks bring the biggest joys. I think every cable should automatically come with a cable tie to stow it away. But that's another topic. 

I'm happy with my solution now.
